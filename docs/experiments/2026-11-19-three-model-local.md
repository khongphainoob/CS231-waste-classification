# Local three-model comparison — 2026-11-19

All three verified Member 2 checkpoints were evaluated on the same 716-image held-out test split (`data/standard_split/test`) on CPU. The timing includes model preprocessing and forward inference, after three warmups; it excludes disk I/O, resize and Grad-CAM.

| Model | Epochs in checkpoint | Accuracy | Macro F1 | Mean latency | FPS | Checkpoint |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| LightWasteCNN | 1 | 62.989% | 63.117% | 11.579 ms | 86.365 | 4.892 MiB |
| ResNet18 | 13 | **93.575%** | **93.549%** | 71.203 ms | 14.044 | 42.723 MiB |
| MobileNetV4 Conv Small | 1 | 76.676% | 76.822% | 36.780 ms | 27.189 | 9.732 MiB |

The two one-epoch runs are smoke experiments to validate the complete train → checkpoint → test pipeline; they are not final model training runs. ResNet18 uses the existing checkpoint selected by validation Macro F1. Do not interpret the accuracy ranking as a tuned three-model study until LightWasteCNN and MobileNetV4 are trained to the planned epoch budgets.

Dataset audit: 3,337 train images, 715 validation images, 716 test images; no exact cross-split duplicates; no synthetic filenames. Dataset manifest SHA-256: `38fe0c6d1f67e073d1402f93e55ed1c5f05d5485ab1fe8d16897326a97839dba`.

The machine had no CUDA device, so all timing is CPU-only.
